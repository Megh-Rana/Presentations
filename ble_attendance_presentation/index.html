BLE Smart Attendance

A mobile attendance system using BLE proximity detection and face recognition to make marking attendance faster, easier, and tamper-proof — without any extra hardware.
Team Project · Megh Rana · Kushagra Upadhyay · Moksh Raval
CHARUSAT · U & P U Patel Dept. of Computer Engineering
01

The Problem

Attendance in colleges today is still handled through slow, manual, or easily-gamed methods. The core issue is that the process wastes valuable class time, burdens instructors and students, and digital alternatives introduce their own problems.

Roll Call

The most common method. It works, but it's time-consuming — every lecture loses several minutes to calling out names or roll numbers. There's also a small chance a student's response goes unheard. The major flaw is purely time.

Time Wasted

Face Scanning Hardware

Automated face scanning solves the time problem, but requires dedicated cameras and an entire setup to be installed in every classroom — expensive to deploy and maintain at scale.

Costly Hardware

QR Code Systems

Faster than roll call, but a QR code or its screenshot can be shared or forwarded — enabling proxy attendance with zero physical presence required.

Spoofable

Biometric Kiosks

Require dedicated hardware at fixed points, expensive to deploy at scale, and create bottlenecks and queues at entry points.

Costly & Bottleneck
02

The Solution

BLE Smart Attendance combines two signals that together are hard to fake and easy to use — solving both speed and integrity at once.

Signal 1

BLE Proximity

The student's phone must be physically near the instructor's phone (acting as a BLE beacon). The app continuously verifies presence using rotating tokens — the student must stay in range for at least 75% of the class duration.

+
Signal 2

Face Recognition

At the end of the lecture, the student verifies their identity using face recognition on their own device. This confirms the phone in proximity was actually used by its owner throughout the session.

Two-factor attendance: Continuous presence + identity verification. The design makes faking attendance inherently harder than just being present — proxy prevention is a natural side effect of good design, not aggressive policing.
03

Why This Matters

The biggest win here is time. The entire attendance process becomes invisible — it happens automatically while teaching continues uninterrupted.

For Students

No more splitting attention between the lecture and waiting for your roll number to be called. No more saying "present" each time. Attendance happens silently in the background while students focus entirely on learning.

For Instructors

No manual headcount, no calling out names or roll numbers, and no responsibility to manage attendance at all. It records automatically. At the end of the day, just download and upload to eGovernance whenever convenient — with one-click Excel export.

For Admins

Real-time dashboard instead of end-of-week Excel compilation. Instant exportable records, searchable history, and live session monitoring. No more manual data entry or transcription errors.

Zero responsibility on anyone: Neither student nor teacher has to actively "take" attendance. The system handles it completely in the background — the only manual step is the face scan at finalization, which takes seconds.
04

How It Works

A one-time login setup on both ends. After that, the app just needs to be opened at the start of the day — everything else is automatic.

1

Teacher Starts Session

Teacher opens the app, selects the session from the list, and taps start. The phone begins broadcasting a BLE signal.

2

Auto-Scanning Begins

Students have the app running in the background. It automatically detects the BLE signal — no manual scanning needed. Attendance tracking starts silently.

3

Continuous Verification

The app keeps rotating tokens throughout the lecture, verifying the student stays in range. Must be present for >75% of class duration.

4

Teacher Finalizes

Lecture ends. Teacher presses "Finalize Attendance" — eligible students (>75% presence) get the option to finalize on their phones.

5

Face Scan & Confirm

Students tap finalize, scan their face through the app. Identity verified — marked present. Takes a few seconds per student.

6

Session Ends & Uploads

Teacher clicks "End Session." Attendance uploads to the server instantly. Accessible via the web dashboard and exportable to Excel.

06

How This Is Better

Compared to existing approaches, BLE + face recognition simultaneously binds attendance to a specific physical location and a specific verified identity — using only phones.

vs. RFID / Card Systems

Cards can be shared or swiped by anyone — no identity verification. BLE + face recognition binds the check-in to the actual person, not a transferable token.

vs. QR Code Systems

QR codes are trivially screenshot-able and forwardable. Our system requires physical BLE proximity (can't be forwarded) + live face recognition (can't be faked with a photo).

vs. Face Scanning Hardware

Classroom face scanners require dedicated cameras in every room — expensive to install and maintain. We use the phones students already carry — zero additional hardware cost.

Active Development with Proven Results

The concept is proven and working — we've had good initial success with the Android build, a live CI/CD pipeline, and a functional end-to-end flow. We are actively developing and iterating, and need funding to test at scale across a real student body.

Two-factor defense: To fake attendance, a proxy would need to remain in BLE range for 75% of the lecture AND defeat live face recognition at finalization. This makes proxy attendance near-impossible without simultaneous device and face spoofing.

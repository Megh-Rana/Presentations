The CRAFT Method
05

Better Prompts = Better Results

The secret to getting great results from AI? Use the CRAFT framework. Garbage in → Garbage out.

C
Context
Give background info — who you are, what the situation is
R
Role
Assign AI a persona — "Act as a nutritionist" or "Explain like I'm 10"
A
Action
Tell exactly what you want it to do — be specific and clear
F
Format
Specify the output shape — table, list, paragraph, code, etc.
T
Tweaks
Add constraints — tone, length, language, what to avoid
CRAFT in Action
✗ Without CRAFT "Tell me about healthy food"
✓ With CRAFT "[C] I'm a first-year college student. [R] Act as a nutritionist. [A] Create a 7-day vegetarian meal plan on ₹200/day. [F] Present as a table with calories. [T] Keep it simple, no exotic ingredients."

Stay Critical
08

The Good, The Bad & The "Huh?"

Advantages
Saves massive amounts of time
Helps you learn faster
Boosts creativity & ideation
Available 24×7, never tired
Helps everyone — beginner to expert
Limitations
Can be confidently wrong (hallucinations)
Can generate fake images & videos
Privacy & data concerns
Makes up facts that sound real
Overdependence can hurt learning
Fun Fact: Ask AI "How many R's in Strawberry?" — it often says 2, even though there are 3! AI is great at sounding confident, even when it's completely wrong. Always verify!
